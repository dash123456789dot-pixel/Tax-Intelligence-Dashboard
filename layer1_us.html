📋
First-Year Election Active — Key Consequences By turning this on, you are choosing to be taxed as a U.S. resident from the date you first arrived this year. This is a formal IRS election with significant implications.
What This Election Does

Normally, if you arrive mid-year, you are a Non-Resident Alien for the days before your arrival. This election lets you choose to be treated as a full-year Resident Alien, which means your worldwide income from your entry date is taxable in the U.S. It is commonly used to allow a couple to file jointly (MFJ) in their first year.

IRS Eligibility Conditions

To qualify, you must: (1) not have been a U.S. resident in the prior year, (2) be present in the U.S. for at least 31 consecutive days during this year, and (3) be present for at least 75% of the days from your first day of arrival to December 31. If these are not met, the election is invalid.

    What is the Section 199A QBI Deduction?
    
                    
    It allows self-employed individuals and owners of pass-through businesses (sole proprietorships, partnerships, S-corps, and trusts) to deduct up to 20% of their net business income from their personal income tax.

    What is a Specified Service Trade or Business (SSTB)?
    
                    
    An SSTB is any business where the principal asset is the reputation or skill of one or more of its owners or employees. If your business is an SSTB, your QBI deduction is phased down above the lower limit and **completely eliminated** above the upper limit.
