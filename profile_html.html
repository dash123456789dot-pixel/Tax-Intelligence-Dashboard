Dual Residency Conflict Detected You are classified as a tax resident under both Indian domestic laws (Fiscal Year: Apr–Mar) and US domestic laws (Calendar Year: Jan–Dec). This creates overlapping worldwide tax exposure.
DTAA Article 4 Tie-Breaker

Under the India-US Treaty, you can resolve dual residency by breaking the tie to one country using these sequential tests: Permanent Home ➔ Center of Vital Interests ➔ Habitual Abode ➔ Nationality. Breaking the tie to the US allows you to file your Indian return as a Non-Resident and claim DTAA benefits.

Tax Year Mismatch & FTC Apportionment

If you do not break the tie, you must claim a Foreign Tax Credit (FTC Form 67 / Section 90) to avoid double taxation in India. Because the Indian Fiscal Year (April–March) crosses two US Calendar Years, you must apportion US calendar-year earnings and withholdings to the corresponding Indian fiscal months.
